Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 25, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. They help sites remember information about your visit.

Cookies used on this site

This site uses Google Analytics, which sets a small number of cookies to count visitors and understand which pages are popular. These cookies do not identify you personally.

  • _ga, _ga_* — set by Google Analytics to distinguish unique users.

No advertising cookies and no marketing or remarketing cookies are used on this site.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics specifically using the official browser add-on. Blocking cookies does not affect your ability to use this site.
